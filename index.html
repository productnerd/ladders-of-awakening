Ladders of Awakening

Forty-three maps of the path, East and West: religious, mystical, philosophical and psychological. Each column climbs from the bottom. Phases at the same height are alike in kind, and the outlined phase is where that map ends. Under each phase is the tradition’s own term, where it has one. Rest on a phase, or tap it, for a short description.

How to read the heights. They are a reading for comparison, not a claim that the traditions agree. Some maps begin high (the Buddhist stages start with a first awakening) and some end lower (Aristotle’s summit is contemplation, not union).

Not shown. Shinto and the Akan tradition of Ghana have no widely used staged map of the path, so they have no column.

Original terms. Greek, Chinese and Japanese are given in their own script, with the romanisation of Chinese and Japanese on hover. Latin, Sanskrit, Pali, Arabic, Persian, Hebrew, Punjabi, Tibetan and Avestan terms are transliterated, with the original script on hover where it has one.

Sources. Rest on a column’s name to see its source and its title in the original. Drafted from the primary texts and standard accounts, for discussion; corrections are welcome.
